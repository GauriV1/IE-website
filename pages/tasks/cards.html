Purchasing Cards (Credit Cards)

Utilize Your Card

Employee Credit Cards – the college utilizes JP Morgan Chase credit cards for employee purchasing. In addition to "brick and mortar" purchases, online purchases are a good way to utilize this payment method. The Division has an Amazon Business account for staff to utilize.

Purchasing Limit - Credit cards are issued to staff with a credit limit of $5,000.00 and a single transaction limit of $3,000.00. For support, please email jpmorgan@whitman.edu.

Important Purchasing "Red Flags"

  • Gift Cards are considered taxable income. For gift cards given to Whitman community members: fill out this gift card form. For non-Whitman community members: fill out the form only if the gift card exceeds $75 per individual.
  • Gifting a non-cash gift (occasional flowers, fruit, books, etc.) to any individual must be reported if it exceeds $75 per individual.
  • Alcohol The Division for Diversity and Inclusion has a one-drink policy. Federal funds cannot be used to pay for alcohol.

Obtain A Receipt

Make sure you get an itemized receipt for each purchase. Missing or lost receipts create additional work and can place your status as an authorized credit card user in jeopardy. Consider capturing your receipt by taking a photograph and saving as a PDF.

Compile Your Receipts

Compile your receipts in date order, ensuring that each receipt is legible and that the date of the purchase, the total purchase, and the last four digits of your credit card are clear. Scan paper copies and save as PDFs; save email receipts as PDFs. Combine all PDF documents into one master document (see Folio).

Review Your Paperwork: Screen, Sum, and Stamp

Review your receipts and transactions for accuracy; if a tip has been added, ensure both tip amount and total amount are included. "Stamp" each receipt with the requisite information (see Stamping Your Paperwork).
