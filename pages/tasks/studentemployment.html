How Student Wages & Your Budget

Each student supervisor should allocate at least $15,000/year for student employees (based upon hiring two students). Each year, WA State minimum wage rates are announced just prior to January 1. The 2023 Minimum Wage was $15.74/hour. The 2024 Minimum Wage is $16.28 per hour. The 2025 Minimum Wage will be $16.66 per hour (as of January 1, 2025).

Onboarding A Student

HR Paperwork

If your student is not already set-up to work on campus, they will need to make an appointment with the Human Resources Department to complete the following three pieces of paperwork, prior to their start date:

  • W-9 Request for Taxpayer Identification Number and Certification
  • Whitman College Authorization Agreement for Electronic Deposit
  • W-4 Employee's Withholding Certificate

They will also need acceptable identification. A passport by itself or both a driver's license and social security card constitute acceptable identification. Please note: original documents are required.

Student Payroll Position Code

You can access the complete list of Student Payroll Position Codes via the Business Office's Student Payroll webpage. A list of Student Payroll Position Codes that have been utilized by The Division for Diversity and Inclusion can be found here.

Student Employment Request Form

You will need to complete the Student Employment Request Form in order to add your student employee to the payroll, as well as to add them to the list of students whose hours you will be approving, via MyWhitman.

You will need: Student's Name, WID (Student's Whitman ID), Position Code, Position Title, Hourly Rate (typically WA State's minimum wage rate), Start Date, work location (if outside WA State), Supervisor's Name, End Date (if known), grant information if applicable, Faculty or Staff status, and your department admin.

Paying A Student

The Student Pay Period

The student pay period is from the 9th of the month to the 8th of the following month. Student payday is on the 20th of the month. Students should enter their time each day they work during the month. Supervisors and students will have one business day after the 8th to complete timesheets.

Tracking Hours Worked (Employee)

Ask your student employee to utilize the Student Time Tracker template. Hours are submitted via Web Time Entry on MyWhitman.

Instructions: Student Instructions, Supervisor Instruction.

If a student does not have any hours to record, please have them record '0' rather than no entry at all.

Changing A Pay Rate

Important Dates - Student Pay Period & Approving Student Hours

The student pay period is from the 9th of the month to the 8th of the following month. Student payday is on the 20th of the month.

  • If the 20th falls on a Saturday, payday would be the Friday before.
  • If the 20th falls on a Sunday, payday will be the following Monday.
  • Students should enter their time each day they work during the month.
  • The student has until 11:59pm one day after the pay period ends and the supervisor has until 11:59 pm two days after the pay period ends.

Please include your Admin Support as a back-up approver if you will be on vacation.

Students may work up to 20 hours per week while classes are in session.
