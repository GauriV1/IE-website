Travel & Meals

Travel, Meals & Entertainment (TME)

TME (Travel, Meals & Entertainment), is a "throw-back" term from before the time of JP Morgan purchasing cards. Anyone who traveled, dined out, or rented a car would pay for these expenses up front - out of their own pocket - and then be reimbursed by the college by submitting TME paperwork. While the Business Office still processes "TMEs" on occasion, this is the exception and not the norm. For the most part, employees charge purchases to their JP Morgan Chase credit cards for airfare, car rentals, meals, and ground transportation.

Travel & Transportation

Airfare — Reserve air travel utilizing your JPMC purchasing card. Whitman College has an Alaska Airlines mileage account (Alaska Airlines EasyBiz); contact your Admin Support to request a user profile.

Fuel & Mileage — Fuel reimbursement for a personal vehicle is prohibited. If driving your personal vehicle you will be reimbursed for mileage according to the current rate. Mileage Reimbursement Rate.

Motor Pool Vehicles — Contact Facilities for motor pool policies and reservations.

Insurance & Liability

If you are booking flights via Alaska Airlines Easybiz, trip protection is automatically included. Our Division policy is not to opt for trip insurance when booking with other airlines.

Ground Transportation - Waivers: If you are a driver or passenger in a personal vehicle, complete the transportation waiver form prior to departure. Liability waivers: Driver | Passenger.
