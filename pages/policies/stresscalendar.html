Student Stress Calendar

Adapted from the "Student Stress Schedule," Illinois State University, Office of Residential Life, RA Handbook and Resident Advisor Training for the Department of Residential Life at Lewis & Clark College.

August/September

  • Homesickness- especially first year students
  • Roommate conflicts; initial adjustment to academic environment
  • Values exploration; campus familiarization
  • Test anxiety; dating anxieties; time management frustration

October

  • Academic stress from midterms; roommate problems continue
  • Economic anxiety; job panic for mid-year graduates

November

  • Academic pressure mounts; economic anxiety (funds running out)
  • Depression and anxiety; roommate problems may emerge again

December

  • Extracurricular time strain; anxiety as finals approach
  • Financial strains; friendship tension with final exams

January

  • Post-holiday depression; anxiety about second semester performance

February

  • Vocational choice anxiety; social calendar; Spring Break planning

March

  • Academic pressure increases; senior job interviewing; living arrangement anxieties

April

  • Papers and exams approach; summer job pressures; registration frustration

May

  • Finals pressures; senior job pressure; depression over leaving friends
